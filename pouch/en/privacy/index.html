Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Pouch is built so that its developer does not collect your personal information. What you write is stored only on your Mac and in your own iCloud, and the developer cannot see it.

What is stored, and where

  • Card text is encrypted on your Mac before it is saved. It exists only in encrypted form, on your Mac and in the private storage of your iCloud account (a CloudKit private database).
  • Card details — which box a card is in, when it was created and last edited, its order, and the identifier of the key that locked it — are stored in the same places without app-level encryption. They are also kept only in your iCloud and are protected by Apple's iCloud security.
  • The encryption key is stored in your iCloud Keychain and travels only to your other Macs. Your recovery key is shown on screen only when you view it in Settings after authenticating; it is never saved anywhere else or sent anywhere.
  • App settings, such as the sort order, stay on that Mac.

Your iCloud private storage can be opened only with your own Apple Account. The developer has no access to its contents.

What is not collected

Pouch contains no analytics, ads, tracking, or third-party SDKs. It does not send your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any other information to the developer, and it does not share or sell personal information to anyone.

If you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in your device settings, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports and usage statistics in a form that does not identify you. This is a feature provided by Apple, not data collected by Pouch.

Deleting your data

  • When you delete a card in the app, it is also deleted from your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app does not remove cards stored in iCloud, so you can pick up where you left off if you reinstall. To remove everything, delete all cards before deleting the app.

Children's privacy

Pouch does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the changes and a new effective date will be posted on this page.
