Frequently asked questions

How do I open the notch?

Hover over the notch to show the input, then click to open the input and your cards. Write a thought and press Return to put it in the Inbox. Even with the main window closed, you can keep using Pouch from the notch and the Pouch icon in the menu bar.

My cards don't show up on my other Mac

Make sure both Macs are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account. Click the cloud icon at the top right of the main window to see the sync status and when it last synced. A newly set up Mac may take a moment to download your cards.

I see "Unreadable Card" or a message about waiting for the key

The encryption key that locked the card hasn't reached this Mac yet. If iCloud Keychain is on (System Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Passwords), your cards unlock automatically as soon as the key arrives. If it's off, turn it on, or enter your saved recovery key under "Enter Recovery Key" in Pouch Settings.

Where do I find my recovery key?

Open Pouch Settings and choose "Show Recovery Key", then authenticate. Without the recovery key your cards can't be read, so write it down and keep it somewhere safe. The developer doesn't know your recovery key and can't recover it for you.

How do I make Pouch open at login?

Turn on "Open at Login" in Pouch Settings. If it doesn't stay on, allow Pouch in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions.

Does Pouch work on a Mac without a notch?

Pouch is designed for MacBooks with a notch. On a screen without one, it shows the same panel at the top center, but this hasn't been fully tested yet. Let us know if anything feels off.

How do I delete my data?

When you delete a card in the app, it's also deleted from your iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for details.
